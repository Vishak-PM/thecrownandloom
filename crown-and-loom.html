Como, Italy — Weaving Floor
The Atelier

Every thread accounted for

Our cloth is woven on century-old looms in Como, where third-generation weavers still judge a bolt of fabric by hand before it ever reaches our cutting tables. Nothing leaves the atelier until it has earned the name.

A single overcoat passes through fourteen pairs of hands — from the first chalk mark to the final, near-invisible stitch along the lapel roll.
